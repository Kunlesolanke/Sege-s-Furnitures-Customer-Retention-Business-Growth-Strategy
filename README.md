Industry: Furniture & Home Furnishing
Focus: Customer Retention, Customer Experience & Sustainable Business Growth

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Sege's Furnitures is a growing furniture business focused on building its customer base and establishing a sustainable position in the market.

As a growing business, attracting new customers is important, but long-term growth also depends on the ability to retain existing customers, encourage repeat purchases, generate referrals, and maintain strong customer relationships.

This Business Analysis project examines how Shakes Furnitures can develop a structured customer-retention and relationship-management approach.

The analysis focuses on identifying potential gaps in the customer journey, understanding the possible causes of customer loss, defining business requirements, and recommending practical improvements that can help the business increase customer retention and sustainable growth.

Note: This case study is being developed as a real-world business analysis project. Specific operational findings will be validated with the business owner before being presented as confirmed findings.

---

## 2. Business Context

Shakes Furnitures is a growing furniture business serving customers looking to purchase furniture products for homes, offices, and other spaces.

Like many growing businesses, the company needs to balance two important objectives:

1. Acquiring new customers.
2. Retaining and developing existing customers.

A customer who makes a single purchase provides immediate revenue. However, a satisfied customer can potentially create additional value through:

- Repeat purchases
- Referrals
- Recommendations
- Positive word-of-mouth
- Long-term customer relationships

Therefore, the project focuses on how Shakes Furnitures can move from a primarily transaction-focused customer relationship toward a more structured and sustainable customer relationship model.

---

## 3. Business Problem

Problem Statement

Seges  Furnitures may face challenges in converting first-time customers into repeat customers and long-term advocates because customer relationships may not yet be supported by a clearly defined retention and post-purchase process.

The business therefore needs to determine:

«What factors influence whether customers return to Shakes Furnitures, and what business processes can be introduced to improve customer retention, repeat purchases, customer satisfaction, and referrals?»

The objective is not simply to increase sales through discounts or promotions, but to identify sustainable ways of creating customer value.

↓

Customer makes enquiry

↓

↓

Customer places order

↓

Furniture is produced/procured

Furniture is delivered

Potential Business Gap

The critical area requiring investigation is what happens after the transaction.

Potential questions include:

- Is the customer contacted after delivery?
- Is customer satisfaction measured?
- Is feedback collected?
- Are complaints recorded and tracked?
- Are previous customers contacted about relevant products?
- Are customers encouraged to refer others?

These questions must be validated with the business before being classified as confirmed findings.

## 7. Root-Cause Analysis

Potential Core Problem

Low or inconsistent repeat purchases

Possible contributing factors include:

Customer Information Gap
Customer information may not be systematically captured and organized.

↓

Limited Customer Follow-Up
Customers may receive little structured communication after completing a purchase.

↓

Limited Feedback Collection
The business may not have a formal process for understanding customer satisfaction.

↓

Complaint Management Gap
Customer complaints may not be consistently recorded, tracked and analyzed.

↓

Limited Customer Re-engagement
Previous customers may not be systematically reminded about relevant products or services.

↓

Missed Referral Opportunities
Satisfied customers may not be actively encouraged to refer new customers.

These are hypotheses to be validated through stakeholder interviews and customer research.

## 8. Customer Journey Improvement Opportunity

Current Model

Awareness → Enquiry → Purchase → Delivery → End

Proposed Model

Awareness → Enquiry → Purchase → Delivery → Follow-Up → Feedback → Relationship Building → Repeat Purchase / Referral

The proposed model extends the relationship beyond the initial transaction.

10. Proposed Future-State (TO-BE) Process

Step 1 — Customer Purchase

Capture relevant customer information during the purchasing process.

↓

Step 2 — Delivery

Ensure the product is delivered according to the agreed requirements.

↓

Step 3 — Post-Purchase Follow-Up

Contact the customer after delivery to confirm satisfaction.

↓

Step 4 — Feedback

Collect customer feedback regarding:

- Product quality
- Delivery experience
- Customer service
- Overall satisfaction

↓

Step 5 — Issue Resolution

If a problem is identified:

Complaint → Record → Assign → Resolve → Confirm Satisfaction → Close

↓

Step 6 — Customer Relationship Building

Maintain appropriate communication with previous customers.

↓

Step 7 — Re-engagement

Provide relevant information about products or services that may be useful to the customer.

↓

Step 8 — Referral / Repeat Purchase

Create opportunities for satisfied customers to return or recommend the business.

## 8. Solution Options

Option 1 — Post-Purchase Follow-Up

Introduce a structured follow-up process after every completed order.

Expected benefit: Improved customer experience and earlier identification of problems.

---

Option 2 — Customer Feedback Process

Introduce a simple mechanism for collecting customer opinions and identifying recurring problems.

Expected benefit: Better understanding of customer needs and service improvement.

---

Option 3 — Customer Referral Programme

Create a structured process for encouraging satisfied customers to refer friends, family and colleagues.

Expected benefit: Additional customer acquisition through existing customers.

---

Option 4 — Customer Re-engagement

Maintain relationships with previous customers through relevant communication.

Expected benefit: Increased opportunities for repeat purchases.

---

Option 5 — Complaint Management Process

Introduce a consistent method for recording, assigning and resolving complaints.

Expected benefit: Improved service recovery and customer satisfaction.

## 10. Recommended Business Decisions

Based on the preliminary analysis, Shakes Furnitures should consider prioritizing the following:

1. Build a structured customer information process

The business should understand who its customers are and maintain sufficient information to support future customer relationships.

2. Introduce post-purchase follow-up

Customer interaction should not end immediately after delivery.

3. Establish a feedback and complaint process

Customer problems should become opportunities for improving the business rather than isolated incidents.

4. Develop a referral strategy

Satisfied customers can become an important source of new customers.

5. Re-engage previous customers

The business should develop appropriate ways of staying connected with existing customers.

## 12. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The following KPIs can help management measure progress:

Customer Retention Rate

Measures the percentage of customers retained over a specific period.

Repeat Purchase Rate

Measures the proportion of customers who make another purchase.

Customer Referral Rate

Measures how many new customers come through existing-customer referrals.

Customer Satisfaction

Measures how satisfied customers are with the product and service.

Complaint Resolution Time

Measures how quickly customer problems are resolved.

Follow-Up Completion Rate

Measures the percentage of completed orders receiving post-purchase follow-up.

Number of Returning Customers

Tracks the number of customers who return to purchase again.
